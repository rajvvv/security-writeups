# Types of Computer Networks

## 1. LAN (Local Area Network)
A LAN connects devices within a small geographical area, such as a home, office, computer lab, or college building.

- **Example:** Computers connected in a college laboratory.
- **Coverage:** Small area.
- **Cybersecurity:** Unauthorized devices connected to a LAN may access shared resources if security is weak.

## 2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
A MAN connects multiple LANs across a city or metropolitan area.

- **Example:** A company connecting its offices across Pune.
- **Coverage:** City or metropolitan area.
- **Cybersecurity:** Requires secure connections between different locations.

## 3. WAN (Wide Area Network)
A WAN connects networks across large geographical areas, such as different cities or countries.

- **Example:** A company connecting its offices in Mumbai, Pune, and Delhi.
- **Coverage:** Large geographical area.
- **Cybersecurity:** VPNs, firewalls, and encryption help protect communication across networks.

## 4. WLAN (Wireless Local Area Network)
A WLAN connects devices within a local area using wireless communication, usually Wi-Fi.

- **Example:** A hostel Wi-Fi network.
- **Coverage:** Local area.
- **Cybersecurity:** Weak Wi-Fi passwords and insecure encryption can allow unauthorized access.

## Quick Comparison

| Network | Full Form | Main Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| LAN | Local Area Network | Building or campus |
| MAN | Metropolitan Area Network | City |
| WAN | Wide Area Network | Countries or continents |
| WLAN | Wireless Local Area Network | Local area using Wi-Fi |

## Remember
- LAN: Local network.
- MAN: City-wide network.
- WAN: Large geographical network.
- WLAN: Local network using wireless communication.

**Note:** WLAN is a type of LAN that uses wireless connections, while LAN can use wired or wireless connections.
